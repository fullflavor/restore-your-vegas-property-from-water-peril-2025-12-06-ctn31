Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our water damage restoration services.

We understand that water damage is a time-sensitive emergency. RestoreVegas maintains a 24/7 emergency response team that can typically arrive at your property within one hour of your call, regardless of the time or day. Our rapid response protocol includes immediate assessment, water extraction, and initial mitigation measures to prevent further damage and mold growth. The faster we arrive, the more we can save of your property and belongings.

Most homeowners insurance policies cover water damage restoration, though coverage depends on the cause of the damage and your specific policy terms. RestoreVegas works directly with your insurance company to maximize your coverage and minimize your out-of-pocket expenses. We handle all documentation, photographs, and detailed estimates required by your insurer. Our team is experienced in navigating insurance claims and can often help you understand what is and isn't covered under your policy.

The duration of restoration depends on the extent of the damage and the areas affected. Minor water damage might be resolved in a few days, while more extensive damage could take weeks. Our typical process includes: water extraction (24-48 hours), drying (3-7 days), inspection and assessment, repairs and restoration, and final walk-through. We provide a detailed timeline during your initial assessment and keep you updated throughout the process. Our goal is to restore your property as quickly as possible without compromising quality.

Mold is a fungus that thrives in moist environments and can begin growing within 24-48 hours of water damage. It poses serious health risks, particularly for children, elderly individuals, and people with respiratory conditions. Mold can cause allergic reactions, asthma attacks, and other respiratory issues. Our comprehensive mold prevention services include moisture control, antimicrobial treatments, and ventilation improvements. Early prevention is far more cost-effective and safer than dealing with extensive mold remediation later. We take mold prevention seriously because your family's health depends on it.

Yes, we stand behind our work with comprehensive warranties. We provide a one-year warranty on all water damage restoration work, covering labor and materials. Additionally, our mold prevention treatments come with a two-year monitoring period, during which we conduct follow-up inspections to ensure continued protection. We also offer extended warranty options for additional peace of mind. Our confidence in our work is backed by years of experience and industry certifications. If any issues arise, we're committed to making them right promptly and professionally.
